Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Blinkora Studio ("Blinkora", "we", "us") is an early-stage product that helps small businesses and online sellers create marketing videos through conversational experiences on the web, on mobile, and on WhatsApp. This policy explains, in plain English, what information we handle, why we handle it, and the choices you have.

Blinkora is currently in an early development / pilot stage. We aim to keep our practices simple and transparent, and we will update this policy as the product grows.

1. Information you may provide

When you use Blinkora, you may share the following with us:

  • WhatsApp phone number and basic profile information made available through WhatsApp (such as your display name), when you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Messages you send to Blinkora, including text, voice notes, and instructions about the content you want.
  • Uploaded files such as product images, videos, logos, and other media or documents.
  • Business, product, and campaign information, such as your business name, product descriptions, prices, target audience, and campaign goals.

We may also collect limited technical information needed to run the service, such as timestamps, message delivery status, and basic logs used for troubleshooting and security.

Please do not send sensitive personal information (for example, health, financial account, or government ID details) that is not needed to create your marketing content.

2. Why we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Understand your requests and respond to you in the conversation.
  • Generate, edit, and deliver marketing videos and related content for you.
  • Store your projects and generated assets so you can review or revise them.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Protect the service and its users against abuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Communicate with you about your requests or important changes to the service.

3. Third-party service providers

To provide the service, we rely on third-party providers. When required to generate your content, your messages, uploaded assets, and related information may be sent to and processed by:

  • AI and video generation providers that create or edit images, video, text, or audio based on your inputs.
  • Messaging platforms, such as WhatsApp (provided by Meta), which carry messages between you and Blinkora. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's and Meta's own terms and privacy policies.
  • Hosting and storage providers that run our servers and store files.

We share only what is reasonably needed for these providers to perform their function. These providers process data under their own terms and policies, and some may process data in countries other than your own.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information only for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the service, support your projects, and meet the purposes described in this policy. When it is no longer needed, we aim to delete or anonymize it.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions for how to make a request. We may keep a minimal amount of information where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, security, or abuse prevention.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, such as restricting access to systems and data and keeping credentials out of public code. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. You can contact us to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you.
  • Request a copy of your information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Object to or ask us to limit certain uses of your information.
  • Stop using the service at any time, for example by no longer messaging Blinkora on WhatsApp.

We will respond to requests within a reasonable time. We may need to verify your identity, for example by confirming the WhatsApp number linked to your conversations, before acting on a request.

8. Children

Blinkora is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the product evolves. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Where changes are significant, we will make reasonable efforts to let you know.

10. Contact
