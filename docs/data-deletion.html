Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 11, 2026

You can ask Blinkora Studio to delete the data associated with your use of our service, including data from conversations with Blinkora on WhatsApp.

How to request deletion

Send an email to blinkorastudio@gmail.com with:

  1. The subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. The WhatsApp phone number (including country code) or other account identifier you used with Blinkora.
  3. Any other details that help us identify your data, such as your business name or the approximate dates you used the service.

Email: blinkorastudio@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

To protect your data, we may ask you to verify that you control the WhatsApp number or account in question (for example, by sending a message from that number) before we act on the request.

What we will delete

Once your request is verified, we will delete or anonymize the applicable:

  • Conversation data, including messages exchanged with Blinkora.
  • Uploaded media, such as product images, videos, and files you sent.
  • Generated assets, such as videos and other content created for you.
  • Account-related information, such as your phone number, profile details, and business or campaign information stored with your account.

We will confirm by email once your request has been processed. We aim to handle requests within a reasonable time, typically within 30 days.

What may be retained

We may keep a minimal amount of information where reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations, maintain security, or prevent fraud and abuse (for example, a record that a deletion request was received and completed). Such records are kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

Data that has already been processed by third-party providers (such as messaging platforms or AI/video generation providers) is subject to those providers' own retention practices. Messages stored on your own device or in your WhatsApp app are controlled by you and are not deleted by this process.

Questions

For more information on how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us at blinkorastudio@gmail.com.
